FAQ

Can someone reply to my drift bottle?

No. Bottles and secret crush notes are zero-return-address by design. The sender field is stripped before Rainbow Bridge delivery.

Why didn’t Magpie Bridge match me this Sunday?

Match previews are gated at about 20%. Continuous participation averages roughly one preview per month. After a successful mutual agree, both sides cool down for four weeks (unlocked on week 5).

Where is my lover relationship stored?

Only on your device. The cloud does not keep a lover field. Local annotations decide whether midnight mirrors and direct messages are accepted.

How do I block or report a note?

Every inbound bottle, crush note, and Magpie preview includes a Block & Report control. Blocking is local — future packets on that channel are ignored.

How do I restore a subscription?

Open Muse → crown → Restore Purchases. Billing is handled by Apple StoreKit.

Refunds

Use Apple’s Report a Problem flow. We cannot issue refunds from inside the app.
