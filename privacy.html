Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Dear Muse: Soul Drift ("Dear Muse", "we", "our") is built so that your longings, lover graph, and Muse memories stay on your iPhone. This policy explains what little we process and what we never see.

1. What stays on your device

Chat history, drift-bottle archives, secret-crush ledgers, Magpie profiles, local annotated relations, and coupled mirror memories are stored in the app sandbox (Application Support/DearMuse/) and optional App Group containers on your device. Relationship truth (LocalAnnotatedRelation) is never authored by a cloud database field.

2. Rainbow Bridge MQTT exchange

When connected, Dear Muse may use a Rainbow Bridge MQTT broker solely as a stateless message exchange and address/identity resolver (S2-DID / six-segment codes). Messages are designed with retain = false. Zero-return packets (bottles and crush notes) omit sender identity. We do not operate a cloud store of chats or lover status.

3. Subscriptions & payments

Subscriptions (Free / Pro / Max) are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We never see or store your payment card details. Purchase and refund policies follow Apple’s App Store terms.

4. Safety & UGC

Inbound notes pass an on-device safety filter. You can Block & Report any foreign channel; blocking is enforced locally. We may receive voluntary report metadata you choose to submit (channel id + reason) to investigate abuse.

5. What we do not collect

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.
  • We do not run third-party advertising SDKs.
  • We do not upload your Muse conversations or lover graph to a content database we control.

6. Children's privacy

Dear Muse is a general-audience romance companion and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the revised version on this page (also reachable as /privacy.html).

8. Contact

Questions: support@dearmuse.space2.world or GitHub Issues.
